📐 Show Your Work - Educational Feature

Press the 📐 button after any calculation to see detailed, visual explanations:

Visual Learning Elements

  • Fraction Bars: Green filled segments show what fractions actually look like
  • Box Grids: Area models demonstrate multiplication visually
  • Place Value Charts: Break down addition and subtraction step-by-step
  • Grouping Models: Division shown as equal groups with visual blocks

Two Teaching Methods

Switch between tabs to see both approaches:

  • Traditional Method: The way most adults learned—convert to improper fractions, find common denominators, multiply straight across
  • Common Core Method: How kids learn today—visual models, breaking numbers into parts, understanding the concepts

All Operations Covered

  • Addition: Common denominators with visual fraction bars
  • Subtraction: Shows borrowing/regrouping when needed
  • Multiplication: Area models break down (whole + fraction) × (whole + fraction)
  • Division: Keep-Change-Flip explained with reciprocals

Works for Everything

  • Mixed numbers (6 1/8 × 2 2/8)

🔧 Calculator Features

Three Big Keypads

Separate keypads for whole numbers, numerators, and denominators. No mode switching, no confusion—just tap what you need.

Glove-Friendly Design

Large buttons that work with work gloves, winter gloves, or bare hands. Built for job sites, not office desks.

Precision Selector

Click to choose from 1/2", 1/4", 1/8", 1/16", 1/32", or 1/64". See live preview of how each precision rounds your result.

Round Mode Toggle

Choose nearest, round up, or round down. Match your rounding to your project requirements.

MM → Inches Converter

Convert millimeters to fractional inches with one tap. No more looking up conversion charts for metric hardware.

Decimal Support

Enter decimals like 2.375 and convert them to fractions automatically. Works both ways—fractions to decimals too.

Scrolling History

See your last calculations at a glance. No more re-doing math because you forgot what you just calculated.

Handles Huge Numbers

Works with numbers up to 9 quintillion without crashing. Won't overflow like other calculators.
