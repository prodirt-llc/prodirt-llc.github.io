⚠️ Lightning Safety First

Strike Analyzer is not a safety device. Flash-to-bang distances are estimates and can be wrong. Never use this app to decide whether it is safe to stay outdoors.

If you can hear thunder, you are close enough to be struck. Follow the National Weather Service guidance: When Thunder Roars, Go Indoors — and stay there until 30 minutes after the last thunder.

FAQ

How far away is the lightning?

Count the seconds between the flash and the thunder — roughly every 5 seconds is a mile (3 seconds per kilometer). Strike Analyzer does the math for you and adjusts for how temperature changes the speed of sound, so it's more accurate than counting in your head.

How does flash-to-bang timing work?

Light reaches you almost instantly; thunder travels about 343 m/s. The delay between seeing the flash and hearing the bang tells you the distance. Tap at each, or let the camera and mic detect them automatically.

Can it tell me if it's safe to be outside?

No — Strike Analyzer is not a safety device and its distances are estimates. If you can hear thunder, you're close enough to be struck. When thunder roars, go indoors.
