They can be substantially wrong due to reaction time, detection error, terrain, multiple strikes, atmospheric conditions, and other factors. Lightning can strike more than 10 miles from a storm — farther than thunder can be heard. If you can hear thunder, you are within striking distance. Follow National Weather Service guidance: When Thunder Roars, Go Indoors, and remain indoors until 30 minutes after the last thunder.
