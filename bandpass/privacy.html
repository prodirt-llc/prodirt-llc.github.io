Privacy Policy

BandPass by ProDirt LLC

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

🔒 BandPass is built with privacy first. All audio is processed live on your device. Nothing is recorded, uploaded, or sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

This privacy policy applies to the BandPass app (the "Application") for mobile devices, created by ProDirt LLC (the "Service Provider"). The Application is provided "AS IS".

Information We Collect

None. BandPass does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The Application has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising, and it does not send any data to the Service Provider or to any third party.

Microphone Access

BandPass requires access to your device's microphone for one purpose only: to analyze incoming audio in real time so it can display the live spectrogram and apply frequency filtering. This audio is processed on your device as it happens. It is not recorded, not saved, and not transmitted to the Service Provider or anyone else. When you close the Application, the audio stream ends and nothing is retained.

On-Device Settings

Any preferences or settings you configure are stored locally on your device only. The Service Provider has no access to them.

No Ads and No Third-Party Tracking

BandPass contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. It does not use AdMob or any advertising network, and it does not access your location.

Google Play

The Application is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google Play Services may process limited technical data as part of normal app distribution, governed by Google's Privacy Policy →. This is standard for all Android apps and is not something the Service Provider collects or controls.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Audio recordings
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavior tracking

Children

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Because BandPass collects no personal data from any user and requires no account, no personal information is gathered from anyone. If you are a parent or guardian with questions, contact us at prodirt.co@gmail.com.

Data Deletion

Because no data leaves your device, you can remove everything associated with the Application by uninstalling it, or by going to Settings → Apps → BandPass → Storage → Clear Data.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the Application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
