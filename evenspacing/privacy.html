Privacy Policy

Even Spacing Calculator by ProDirt LLC

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

🔒 Even Spacing is built with privacy first. It's a calculator — everything you enter stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

This privacy policy applies to the Even Spacing Calculator app (the "Application") for mobile devices, created by ProDirt LLC (the "Service Provider"). The Application is provided "AS IS".

Information We Collect

None. Even Spacing does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The Application has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising, and it does not send any data to the Service Provider or to any third party.

Permissions

Even Spacing does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. It needs no special device permissions to perform its calculations.

On-Device Data

The only data the Application works with is the measurements and settings you enter. This information is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to the Service Provider or any third party.

No Ads and No Third-Party Tracking

Even Spacing contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. It does not use AdMob or any advertising network.

Google Play

The Application is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google Play Services may process limited technical data as part of normal app distribution, governed by Google's Privacy Policy →. This is standard for all Android apps and is not something the Service Provider collects or controls.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavior tracking
  • Any information shared with third parties

Children

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Because Even Spacing collects no personal data from any user and requires no account, no personal information is gathered from anyone. If you are a parent or guardian with questions, contact us at prodirt.co@gmail.com.

Data Deletion

Because no data leaves your device, you can remove everything associated with the Application by uninstalling it, or by going to Settings → Apps → Even Spacing → Storage → Clear Data.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the Application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
