Every contractor gets asked "what should I charge for this?" The honest answer is: it depends on your costs. Your labor rate, your equipment, your overhead — not some industry average someone posted on Reddit.

FieldQuote makes that calculation concrete and personal. Set up your cost profile once. Every quote after that uses your actual numbers.

How It Works

Enter your real costs once in your Cost Profile. From that point on, every job estimate is automatically calculated using your numbers.

True Cost/hr = Labor Rate + Equipment Cost/hr + Overhead/hr // What it actually costs you to run for an hour Job Cost = Σ(line item costs) + Mobilization // What the job costs you before a cent of profit Floor Price = Job Cost × (1 + your margin%) // Your pre-tax price — or your minimum job fee, whichever is higher Suggested Price = Floor Price + tax // Slide the margin, watch both numbers move live

The margin slider lets you adjust in real time — push it up for a difficult site, pull it back for a repeat client. Your job cost is shown separately and never moves, so you can always see exactly how much of the quote is profit and how close you are to working for free.

How to Price a Job So You Actually Make Money

The most expensive mistake a solo contractor makes is quoting off a gut number. Gut numbers cover materials and the hours you can see, and quietly leave out the overhead that runs whether you are working or not. Here is the arithmetic FieldQuote runs for you every time you build a job.

1. Your true cost per hour = your labor rate + your overhead per hour. Overhead is insurance, truck payment, fuel, phone, software and licenses — divided across the hours you actually bill, not the hours in a week.

2. Job cost = (true cost per hour × hours) + materials + equipment time + the drive to site.

3. Floor price = job cost with your margin applied. This is the number you do not go under.

4. Quoted price = floor price + tax.

The part that matters on site is step 3. When a client pushes back on price, you need to know instantly how far down you can actually go. FieldQuote puts a margin slider on the job and recalculates live as you drag it — the floor price stays fixed on screen the whole time, so you can negotiate knowing exactly where the edge is instead of finding out three days into the work.

Set your rates once in the cost profile and every job after that prices off your real numbers, not an industry average that has never seen your truck payment.

Work from your own cost per hour rather than a guess or an industry average. Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FieldQuote is built on a simple principle: your business data is yours. We do not collect it, store it, or sell it.

Data Storage

All data you enter — cost profiles, jobs, quotes, client information — is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. Nothing is transmitted to ProDirt LLC servers because there are none.

Google Drive Backup

If you choose to enable backup in Settings, FieldQuote will back up your data to your personal Google Drive using the drive.file scope. This means:

  • Backups go into a "FieldQuote Backups" folder in your own My Drive, where you can open, download, or delete them yourself — deliberately visible rather than hidden, so your data stays reachable even if you ever remove the app
  • The drive.file scope restricts FieldQuote to files it created — it cannot read anything else in your Drive
  • ProDirt LLC has no access to your Google Drive or backup files
  • Backup is entirely opt-in — the app functions fully without it

Google Calendar

Scheduling a won job creates an all-day event on your primary Google Calendar using the calendar.events scope. If the job has a client email saved, that client is added to the event and Google sends them the invitation. Scheduling only happens when you tap Schedule Job, and unscheduling removes the event. ProDirt LLC never sees your calendar.

Documents You Attach

Insurance certificates and warranty PDFs you import are copied into FieldQuote's private storage on your device. They are embedded into the quote PDFs you generate and share — that is the point of attaching them — but they are never uploaded anywhere by the app. Google Drive backups carry your typed records, not your imported files.

Location & Address Data

FieldQuote uses your device location (with your permission) to auto-fill your current position when calculating job distances. Address autocomplete and distance calculation use the OpenRouteService API — your search queries are sent to OpenRouteService's servers to return results. No query data is retained by ProDirt LLC.

Internet Permissions

The app requires internet access for: address autocomplete, distance calculation, Google Drive backup, Google Calendar scheduling, and the community template library. All of these are optional — the core quoting functionality, including generating and sharing PDFs, works entirely offline.

Third-Party Services

  • OpenRouteService: Used for address autocomplete and drive distance calculation. Subject to their privacy policy at openrouteservice.org.
  • Google Drive API: Used for optional backup. Subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Calendar API: Used for optional job scheduling. Subject to Google's privacy policy.
