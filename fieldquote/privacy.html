FieldQuote

Privacy Policy
Effective Date: March 18, 2026

ProDirt LLC ("we", "us", or "our") built FieldQuote as a free Android application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use FieldQuote. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

All data you enter into FieldQuote — including your business name, contact information, cost profile settings, job details, client names, and task presets — is stored locally on your device using a secure on-device database. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to back it up to Google Drive.

Google Drive Backup (Optional)

If you choose to use the backup feature, FieldQuote will request access to a private, app-specific folder in your Google Drive account (drive.appdata scope). This folder is not visible in your regular Google Drive and can only be accessed by FieldQuote. Backup files contain your jobs, quotes, cost profile, and task library. We do not have access to your Google Drive or your backup files.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide FieldQuote's features:

  • Calculating job costs and suggested prices based on your cost profile
  • Generating professional quote PDF documents
  • Storing your job history and client information locally on your device
  • Backing up and restoring your data to/from your personal Google Drive

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored locally on your Android device using Android's Room database. Data is stored in the app's private storage area, which is not accessible to other apps. If you enable Google Drive backup, your backup files are stored in a hidden app-specific Drive folder using Google's secure infrastructure. We do not operate any servers and have no access to your data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. The only external services involved are:

  • Google Drive API — used only for optional backup at your request, governed by Google's Privacy Policy
  • Android Share Sheet — when you choose to share a PDF quote, your device's native sharing system is used. We do not control where you send that PDF.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete any job, quote, or task preset at any time from within the app
  • Uninstall the app at any time, which removes all locally stored data
  • Sign out of Google Drive at any time from the Settings screen to disconnect backup access
  • Delete your backup file from Google Drive via Google Drive Settings > Manage Apps

6. Children's Privacy

FieldQuote is intended for use by adult contractors and business owners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and reflected in the updated effective date above. Continued use of FieldQuote after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
