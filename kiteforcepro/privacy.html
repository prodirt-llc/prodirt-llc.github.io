Kite Force – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-11-26  |  Last updated: 2026-09-21

This policy covers both versions of Kite Force, made by ProDirt LLC ("we", "us"):

  • the Android app on Google Play, and
  • the web app at kiteforce.web.app, used in a browser or added to an iPhone home screen.
The short version
  • No ads, no advertising ID, and we never sell your data.
  • Your kites stay on your device, unless you back them up to your own Google Drive (Android) or sign in to the web app to save them to your account.
  • Your location is only used to look up the weather, and is sent to the weather service for that.
  • Neither version uses analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete a web account and every kite in it from Settings, at any time.

Information we collect and why

Your kites

The kites you save (names, shape, measurements, wind rating, notes, line strength, repair notes and any photo you attach) are used to calculate pull and line size. Where they're kept depends on how you use Kite Force:

  • Android app: on your phone. If you turn on Google Drive backup, a copy (photos included) goes to a folder in your own Google Drive. We can't see it.
  • Web app without an account: in your browser's storage on that device only.
  • Web app with an account: in Google Cloud Firestore (United States), under your account, so they're available on every device you sign in on. Security rules let only your account read or change them. Photos are shrunk before they're saved.

Your account (web app only, optional)

If you create an account, Google Firebase Authentication stores your email address. With email sign-in, your password is stored by Firebase in hashed form, and we never see it. With "Continue with Google", Firebase receives the name, email address and profile picture link from your Google account. We use your email only to sign you in and to send account emails you ask for, such as a password reset. The Android app has no accounts.

Location

Kite Force uses your location to fetch live wind, temperature and air pressure for where you are. Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. On Android, they're also sent to Windy to draw the wind map. The web app only asks for your location when you tap "My location". If you search for a place instead, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo's place search. The last place you used is remembered on your device so the weather loads faster next time. We don't keep a location history, and your location is never sent to us.

Photos and camera

You can attach a photo to a kite with your camera or photo library. The photo is stored with that kite, in the places described under "Your kites" above. Kite Force doesn't access any other photos.

When you add a photo, Kite Force may read the date it was taken from the photo itself, on your device, to fill in "Spotted" on a wishlist kite. It doesn't read the photo's location.

Identify with Google Lens is only used when you tap it. It sends that one photo, at full size, to Google Lens (the Google app) or to an app you pick from your phone's share menu. From then on, Google's privacy policy applies to it. Kite Force keeps the full-size copy only while the kite editor is open; the kite itself keeps the smaller saved photo.

Analytics

Neither the Android app nor the web app uses analytics, advertising IDs or tracking. (Android versions before 1.48 included Google Analytics for Firebase; it was removed in 1.48.)

Purchases (Android app only)

The one-time Unlimited upgrade (unlimited kites and Google Drive backup) is sold through Google Play. Google handles all payment details. We never see your card number or billing address, only whether the upgrade has been purchased.

Flight session logs (Android app only)

Sessions you record (conditions over time and any pull readings you enter) are stored on your phone. They only leave it if you choose to share or export them.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services above. The only exception is when the law requires it, for example a valid court order.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Web account: go to Settings → Delete account. This immediately deletes your account and every kite saved in it. If Firebase asks you to sign in again first, that's a safety check.
  • Web app without an account: clearing the site's data in your browser removes your kites.
  • Android app: uninstalling removes everything stored on your phone. Drive backups stay in your Google Drive until you delete the "KiteForcePro Backups" folder.

You can also email prodirt.co@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted. We'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Security

Everything Kite Force sends travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are handled and hashed by Firebase Authentication. The web app's database rules only allow each account to read and write its own kites. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect what we hold.

Children

Kite Force is for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created a web account, email prodirt.co@gmail.com and we'll delete it.
