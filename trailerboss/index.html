FAQ

Can Trailer Boss help me learn to back up a trailer?

Yes. The hitch physics are a real articulated model, so the muscle memory transfers — small corrections early, hand at the bottom of the wheel, watch the mirror the trailer drifts toward. Assist mode shows where to steer while you learn, and every mistake is free.

What's the trick to backing a boat trailer down a ramp?

Put your hand at the bottom of the wheel and move it the way you want the trailer to go, correct small and early, and watch the mirror on the side it's drifting toward. Trailer Boss drills exactly this — with and without a crowd watching.

Does it have real trailer physics?

Yes — a deterministic articulated model with five trailer types that each behave differently. Short trailers dart and jackknife; long fifth-wheels lag and track wide, just like real life.
